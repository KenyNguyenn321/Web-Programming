An ID selector has greater specificity than a class selector and an element

Actual Result After Testing

The #cascade-target rule won. The Computed panel displayed the color as
rgb(106, 27, 154). The .cascade-sample and p color declarations were
overridden because they had lower specificity.

Reflection

The browser result matched my prediction because the ID selector had the
highest specificity. The lower-specificity rules still matched the paragraph,
but the browser crossed out their color declarations in the Styles panel.
