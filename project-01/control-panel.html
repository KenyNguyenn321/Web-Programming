Graduate Bonus Feature

AI Ethics and Safety Notice System

These notices explain risks and mitigations for the simulated forecast interface shown in the Battle Log.

AI

Responsible use notice

The dashboard presents sample forecast percentages as an interface demonstration. The values are not generated by a real AI model and should not replace human safety review.

High Risk 01

Automated match approval

A forecast could incorrectly suggest that a robot is safe or ready for competition.

Mitigation

Require an operator to review power, maintenance, and safety records before approving a match.

Medium Risk 02

Incomplete performance data

Forecasts based only on wins and losses may overlook repairs, arena conditions, or recent equipment changes.

Mitigation

Display the factors behind every forecast and label uncertain or incomplete information.

Low Risk 03

Overreliance on percentages

Operators may treat a high percentage as a guarantee instead of an estimate with limitations.

Mitigation

Pair each percentage with a confidence label, explanation, and visible limitations notice.

Required Review

Human decision-making remains necessary

Final match approval should always come from a trained operator who has reviewed current robot and arena conditions.
